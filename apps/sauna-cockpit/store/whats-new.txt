Version 0.2.1 - 2026-10-18
- Rest no longer goes dark by default: both phases dim, and a fourth all-black page (UP/DOWN) turns the screen off on demand. The Dark screen setting is gone (a stored Dark reads as Dim).
- Undo window for a phase switch shortened to 3 s, so FIT laps land at most 3 s late.

Version 0.2.0 - 2026-10-18
- Settings on the watch: MENU (hold UP), or Settings in the pause menu. A running session takes new targets and limits at once.
- Undo a phase switch: press BACK again within 10 s. The FIT lap is written once that window closes, so a mistaken press leaves no extra round.
- Screen per phase between button presses: dimmed during heat, dark during rest by default (settings: Normal, Dim, Dark). A button, touch or alert brightens it for 10 s.
- Distinct vibrations: three short taps for a phase timer, two long buzzes for the temperature pre-warning, long-short-long for the temperature and HR alarms.
- Round tables with more than four rounds page through them by themselves.
- HR rise (peak minus start HR) per heat round replaces the time above 120 bpm. New lap fields lap_hr_rise and lap_hrr60.
- Fix: Body Battery and stress fields stayed empty when the newest sample had no value.

Version 0.1.1 - 2026-10-18
- Fix: Discard left a black screen, and BACK then reopened the pause menu, so the app could not be left. Save, Discard, Delete and Clear history now return to the right screen.

Version 0.1.0 - 2026-10-18
- Watch app with alternating heat and cool-down phases (BACK switches, each phase a FIT lap), countdown with overtime, bezel progress ring, touch ignored.
- Live HR in zone colour with per-phase low / average / high, watch temperature, total time.
- Alerts: end of heat / cool-down, a watch-temperature pre-warning (default 42 C, cover the watch) and alert (default 44 C, leave), heart rate; vibrate, beep, screen light.
- Round tables with columns sized to their content: heat (time, avg / max HR, peak temperature, time above the effect HR, default 120 bpm) and rest (time, avg / min HR, 60 s HR recovery, temperature drop).
- HRV (RMSSD) per heat and rest phase from beat-to-beat intervals.
- Summary pages Heat / Rest / Recovery; Recovery shows Body Battery at start and end and stress before the session, and after it when recorded as Meditation.
- Record as setting: Other (default, calories counted) or Meditation (stress measured during the session, as Sauna Timer does).
- On-watch history of 50 sessions with 7- and 30-day totals; delete one or clear all.
- Saved as an activity named Sauna with developer fields for Garmin Connect; GPS stays off.
