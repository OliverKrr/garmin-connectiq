Version 0.1.1 - 2026-11-02
- Fix: 0.1.0 crashed when an upload started, so right after a recording and then on every start. Its request parameters were numbers, which Connect IQ rejects with a non-JSON body.
- No crash loop: if the app stops during an upload, the next start shows where it stopped ("stopped at ...") and uploads only from MENU, Upload now.
- Upload and LTE errors show on the status line instead of closing the app.
- Spaces or line breaks pasted into the URL or token settings are ignored.

Version 0.1.0 - 2026-11-02
- First beta: START records a note (Ogg Opus, 16 kHz mono), START or BACK stops it.
- Notes upload in pieces to the server in the settings, through the phone or over the watch's LTE, and stay on the watch until the server confirms the whole note.
- Waiting notes retry in the background every 15 minutes and after each activity.
- MENU: upload now, or delete the notes still waiting.
