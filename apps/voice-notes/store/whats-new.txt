Version 0.3.1 - 2026-11-07
- A recording can be discarded: BACK, DOWN or a tap on the red X asks "Discard recording?"; yes deletes it from the watch so it never uploads, no keeps recording. START or the green check saves.
- While recording, a green check by START and a red X by BACK show what the buttons do.

Version 0.3.0 - 2026-11-07
- MENU, Settings on the watch: keep uploaded notes (days, watch space), retry interval, upload over LTE, longest note. They change the same settings as the phone app.
- New setting: retry waiting notes every 5 to 60 minutes (default 15).

Version 0.2.1 - 2026-11-03
- Upload results name their route (phone or LTE) and keep the error code.
- A dropped connection or a timeout is retried twice at once before an upload gives up.
- Playback errors show their code, and one that ended the app shows on the next start.

Version 0.2.0 - 2026-11-03
- Past notes on the watch: DOWN and UP step through them from the record screen, START plays one through the speaker, MENU deletes it. The app always opens on the record screen.
- Uploaded notes stay for playback for 7 days and within 1 MB (both settings); the oldest go first. Notes not uploaded yet are never deleted automatically.
- Glance in the widget loop: how many notes wait.
- The background retry stays out while the app is open.

Version 0.1.1 - 2026-11-02
- Fix: 0.1.0 crashed when an upload started, so right after a recording and then on every start. Its request parameters were numbers, which Connect IQ rejects with a non-JSON body.
- No crash loop: if the app stops during an upload, the next start shows where it stopped ("stopped at ...") and uploads only from MENU, Upload now.
- Upload and LTE errors show on the status line instead of closing the app.
- Spaces or line breaks pasted into the URL or token settings are ignored.

Version 0.1.0 - 2026-11-02
- First beta: START records a note (Ogg Opus, 16 kHz mono), START or BACK stops it.
- Notes upload in pieces to the server in the settings, through the phone or over the watch's LTE, and stay on the watch until the server confirms the whole note.
- Waiting notes retry in the background every 15 minutes and after each activity.
- MENU: upload now, or delete the notes still waiting.
