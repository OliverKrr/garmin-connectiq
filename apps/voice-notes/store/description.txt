Record a voice note on the watch, mid-run or anywhere, and find it later as audio and text on your
own server. Open source: https://github.com/OliverKrr/garmin-connectiq

This app needs a server that receives the notes. It is built for one, a small service you run
yourself; without it the app records but has nowhere to send. Set the upload URL and token in the
app settings (Connect IQ app on the phone).

HOW IT WORKS
- START records, START or BACK stops. The note is saved on the watch at once.
- It uploads through the phone when one is connected, else over the watch's LTE if the setting
  allows it and the watch has a plan.
- A note leaves the watch only after the server confirms the whole recording, so nothing is lost
  when the connection drops.
- Notes that could not go up retry in the background every 15 minutes and after every activity.
- MENU: upload now, or delete the notes still waiting.

SETTINGS
- Upload URL and upload token: from your server.
- Upload over LTE when no phone is connected (default on).
- Upload piece size in KB (default 16).
- Longest note in minutes (default 10).

Requires a watch with a microphone and Connect IQ 6.0.3 (fenix 9 Pro).
