Record a voice note on the watch, mid-run or anywhere, and find it later as audio and text on your own server. Open source: https://github.com/OliverKrr/garmin-connectiq

This app needs a server that receives the notes. It is built for one, a small service you run yourself; without it the app records but has nowhere to send. Set the upload URL and token in the app settings (Connect IQ app on the phone); the other settings can also be changed on the watch (MENU, Settings).

HOW IT WORKS
- START records, START again stops and saves (green check). BACK or DOWN (red X) asks "Discard recording?" and keeps recording until you answer.
- It uploads through the phone when one is connected, else over the watch's LTE if the setting allows it and the watch has a plan.
- A note leaves the watch only after the server confirms the whole recording, so nothing is lost when the connection drops.
- Notes that could not go up retry in the background every 15 minutes (a setting) and after every activity.
- DOWN and UP step through past notes; START plays one on the watch's speaker. The app always opens on the record screen.
- Uploaded notes stay on the watch for playback for 7 days and within 1 MB (settings); notes not uploaded yet are never deleted automatically.
- A glance in the widget loop shows how many notes wait.
- MENU: upload now, delete the notes still waiting, change settings, or delete the note on screen.

SETTINGS
- Upload URL and upload token: from your server.
- Upload over LTE when no phone is connected (default on).
- Upload piece size in KB (default 16).
- Longest note in minutes (default 10).
- Keep uploaded notes on the watch for N days (default 7; 0 = delete right after upload).
- Watch space for notes in KB (default 1024).
- Retry waiting notes every N minutes (default 15, 5 to 60).

Requires a watch with a microphone and Connect IQ 6.0.3: fenix 8 and 9 (all sizes, Pro and Solar), Forerunner 570 and 970, Venu 4, Venu X1, D2 Air X15, MARQ Gen 3, D2 Mach 2 and 2 Pro, Approach S72. On Venu 4, Venu X1 and Approach S72 (two buttons), swipe up and down step through notes and a long press opens the menu.
