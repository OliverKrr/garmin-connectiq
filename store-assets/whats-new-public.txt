Version 1.3.0 - 2026-10-18
- Grade-adjusted pace (GAP): a new Pace / power option shows current, lap and average GAP, coloured by your pace zones - the flat pace your effort on a hill is worth, an alternative to power on hilly runs. Calibrated to Garmin's own GAP.

Version 1.2.0 - 2026-10-15
- 12 more supported watches (95 in total): the whole fenix 9 family (fenix 9, fenix 9 Pro incl. inReach, fenix 9 Pro Solar), D2 Delta, Descent Mk1 and Forerunner 645 Music.

Version 1.1.0 - 2026-09-30
- 34 more supported watches (83 in total), including Forerunner 70, Venu X1, Venu 2 and Venu Sq 2, Instinct 3 AMOLED, D2, Descent, Approach, fenix 5 Plus and MARQ gen 1.
- Rectangular screens (Venu Sq 2, Venu X1) now use the full screen width.

Version 1.0.0 - 2026-07-11
- First public release: full-screen running data field with zone-coloured pace and power, a heart-rate time-in-zone chart, terrain-adaptive pace/power switching, and selectable pace-zone models (80/20 Run, Joe Friel, CTS, MyProCoach, or custom).
